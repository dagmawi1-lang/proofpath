# STEM2Connect AI Hackathon rules checked November 4, 2026

- Hacking period: October 11 through November 21, 2026.
- Submission deadline: November 21, 2026 at 11:59 PM Eastern.
- Demo Day: November 24, 2026; live or prerecorded presentation is allowed.
- Eligibility: enrolled students aged 13+; guardian permission is required for participants under the legal age of majority. Solo or teams up to six; each participant may belong to one team.
- One project per team. Project must be working and address one of Education, Healthcare, Sustainability, Scientific Research, or Community Impact.
- Required submission: public GitHub repo with README and AI/tool disclosure, demo video up to six minutes, completed Devpost description, primary track, and all team members.
- AI coding assistants are permitted with disclosure. An integrated AI feature is strongly recommended but not mandatory.

**Project track:** Education. ProofPath is a research-process organizer, not a fact-checking authority.

Official rules: https://stem2connect-online-hackathon.devpost.com/rules
