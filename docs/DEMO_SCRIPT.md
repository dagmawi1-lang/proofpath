# ProofPath demo script — about 3 minutes (v1.3.0, limit is 6 minutes)

1. **Problem (20s):** Saving links is easy. Tracking the evidence behind a claim, and noticing when every source you saved agrees with you, is harder.
2. **Library (30s):** Open the fictional demo source. Walk the 8-point checklist and point out that the ring measures research-process completeness, not truth.
3. **Add a source (30s):** Add one with a title, HTTPS link, claim, author, date, and stance "Challenges the claim". Show that a `javascript:` link is rejected.
4. **Claim Map (40s):** Switch to Claim Map. Show the support/challenge/context bar and the "Next research steps" prompts on a claim that has only one supporting source.
5. **Citations (20s):** Switch between APA and MLA, copy a citation, and export the bibliography.
6. **Coach (25s):** Click Review gaps. Say whether it's the local checklist or the configured AI. The card states which one it is.
7. **Portability (15s):** Export the library, then import it again.
8. **Limits (10s):** ProofPath does not fact-check, rank publishers, or prove a source is reliable. Citations are simplified and should be checked against a style guide.

Only describe AI as active if `.env` has a key and the coach card says "Generated by the configured AI provider".
